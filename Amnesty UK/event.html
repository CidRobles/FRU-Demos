The Big Half Marathon

Amnesty International UK
02 Apr 2025, 01:04pm

Run the Big Half Marathon for human rights

Sign up to a charity place in the The Big Half Marathon today. Experience the iconic sights of London as the route takes you past Tower Bridge to the legendary Cutty Sark in Greenwich. You'll run part of the famous London Marathon route on fully closed roads and enjoy the vibrant festival atmosphere at the finish line.

Celebrate London's diversity at this inclusive event, featuring amazing food, music, and entertainment for the whole family. Don't miss the chance to be part of this unique experience!

Date: Sunday 7 September 2025
Distance: 13.1 miles / 21km
Location: London
Fundraising pledge: £350
Registration fee: £30
Minimum age: 17

Sign up for a charity place

Registration is now open for The Big Half 2025 charity places. Complete the registration form today to secure your place in the race with Amnesty International UK.

I have my own place

If you've already acquired a place in the event, join our team as an Own Place runner. There's no minimum fundraising target, just raise as much as you can. 

Signed up to run? Get fundraising!

Kick off your fundraising by setting up an Amnesty International UK online fundraising page using the button below:  

How we’ll support you

As a member of our team you’ll receive:

  • Access to training plans & running support
  • A Team Amnesty running vest with option to personalise
  • An inspiring fundraising pack filled with advice and ideas
  • Dedicated staff member with regular email updates on the event and human rights
  • Fundraising and event materials like collecting tins, posters, badges etc
  • We are always on hand by email or phone to offer further support   

Get in touch

If you have any questions or queries, please contact our Community and Events Fundraising Team who will be able to provide you with further information. We will be more than happy to assist.

Email: team@amnesty.org.uk

While you’re here…

Your freedom and your rights are under threat - right here in the UK and across the world. Governments worldwide are imposing oppressive laws and cracking down on our freedoms, but together, we can fight back.

Like many not-for-profit organisations, we are facing new challenges due to the cost of living crisis and the aftermath of the pandemic. But our mission to protect human rights remains. Every pound you give empowers us to speak out and challenge these injustices. Your donation, however big or small, is valuable.

Please donate today and chip in to help protect human rights. Thank you.
