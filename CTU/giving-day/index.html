About

At Catholic Theological Union, our greatest strength is the unity of our community. Like a family rooted in shared purpose, CTU thrives when students, faculty, staff, alumni, and friends come together in faith, service, and generosity.

This Giving Day 2025, we invite you to be part of that strength. Your donation will directly support scholarships for deserving students, enhance our academic and formation programs, and expand our outreach to communities near and far.

Together, we can ensure that CTU continues to be a place where unity, compassion, and excellence not only flourish—but shape future leaders for the Church and the world. Join us in making a lasting impact. Give today.
