Empowering Future Ministers

Every year, promising students come to CTU with a calling to serve but face financial barriers. Your scholarship support ensures that economic circumstances don't prevent called individuals from pursuing their theological education.

A $1,000 donation provides one month of essential support for a student, covering books, meals, and living expenses while they focus on their studies and spiritual formation.

Supporting World-Class Faculty

Our faculty are renowned scholars and practitioners who bring both academic excellence and pastoral wisdom to the classroom. Your support helps attract and retain exceptional educators who shape the next generation of religious leaders.

Faculty development funds support research, sabbaticals, and continuing education that keeps CTU at the forefront of theological education.
