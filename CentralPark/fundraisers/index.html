Fundraisers


Central Park isn’t just a destination—it’s the backyard, the sanctuary, and the soul of New York City. But keeping 843 acres of green space thriving for 42 million annual visitors takes more than just sunshine; it takes a community.

Whether you’re a morning runner, a weekend picnicker, or a lover of the Great Lawn, you can help protect the landscapes that make NYC iconic. Start a fundraiser today to fund essential care, from planting seasonal blooms to maintaining the historic bridges we all love.

Every dollar ensures that the Park remains a free, beautiful escape for everyone, forever.
