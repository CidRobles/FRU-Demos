Who we are

A driving passion to feed the world.

Whether it’s feeding children, empowering women, training farmers, responding to disasters, or serving a community in need, Convoy of Hope’s goal is to bring help and hope where they are needed most. Convoy has a wide network of volunteers and partners helping us make the world a better place.

Initiatives

Real solutions for real needs.

Nothing at Convoy of Hope happens without purpose. As a nonprofit humanitarian organization, Convoy works alongside communities to bring solutions to the root causes of poverty and hunger.

Convoy of Hope’s strategically designed programs unlock capacity for growth and are fueled by kindness. They are multifaceted. Intentional. Life-changing.
