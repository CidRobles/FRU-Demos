Note from our Founder

Donald Trump and fellow Republicans want us paralyzed, silenced, defeated. And let's be honest: we are not that easily broken. Not you, not me, not any of us who believe in the power of people standing together.

The lesson is clear: Democratic campaigning needs to evolve. It’s time to move beyond the traditions, beyond campaigns that fade after Election Day. We need a movement that lasts — a movement that thrives — and that is exactly what Forward Blue is meant to be.

With all my hope,
