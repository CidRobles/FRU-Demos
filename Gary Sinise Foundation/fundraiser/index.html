Community Fundraisers

If you are interested in showing your gratitude to our nation's defenders by supporting the Gary Sinise Foundation in your own community, please fill out the application in the link below to support the Foundation on your event. While we wish we could work with each Grateful American who is creating events in their own community, please note that we must review each application carefully and are not always able to participate directly.

Given the volume of these requests, please answer the complete form below and we will get back to you within two weeks. As Gary says, "While we can never do enough to show gratitude to our nation's defenders, we can always do a little more." Thank you for wanting to do a little more!
