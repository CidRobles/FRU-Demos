The Realities of Youth Have Changed

Every day, this generation faces:

  • Battles with loneliness, depression and anxiety
  • A lack of mentors to provide guidance in faith and life
  • Feeling unprepared and uncertain about life after graduation
  • The compulsion to compare themselves to friends on social media
  • Being the only Christian in their class
  • Culture that insists their worth depends on studies, salary and success

Every day InterVarsity helps campers, students and emerging adults discover the hope that Jesus offers through each stage of growing up.
