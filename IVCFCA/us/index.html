Why Partner With Us?

Today’s generation of youth and young adults feel unprepared and uncertain about life. They are looking for hope and purpose, but often don’t know where to find it. They long for mentors who will help them discover the answers to tough questions.

  • How do I cope with anxiety when the world is so uncertain?
  • How do I figure out the purpose of my life?
  • Does the Bible have anything relevant to say about life?

Where We Meet Youth

From high school to university to the workplace – these are significant transitions in a young person’s life. Often, it’s during these times that they question their purpose, faith and future. Canadian InterVarsity Christian Fellowship, Inc. supports Christian ministries that step into these spaces and help youth thrive through each transition and beyond.

Partner With Us

What We See

Through ministry work on campus, at camp and in the workplace, we see youth and young adults:

  • Discover Jesus
  • Live undivided lives
  • Think Christianly
  • Act globally
  • Grow in influence

Let’s partner together in the work of helping more young people across North America discover the hope and purpose that Jesus offers.
