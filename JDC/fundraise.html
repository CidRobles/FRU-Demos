Start a Jewish Fundraiser with JDC

You can do your part to make the world better by fundraising for JDC’s global humanitarian work — helping to lift lives and strengthen communities in 70 countries. It’s easy to start your own fundraiser or find an existing one to support. View the options below and see how you or your organization can get involved.

And you can give with confidence! JDC has the exemplary four-star rating from Charity Navigator and Platinum rating from Candid.

FAQs

Where do the funds go?

The funds you raise will go to the area that you have selected for your campaign and promoted in your fundraiser. JDC has been awarded the highest ratings from Charity Navigator, Candid’s Guidestar, and Charity Watch as well as being an accredited charity by the Better Business Bureau, so know that you can give with confidence.

If you are raising funds for Global Jewish 9-1-1, the money supports JDC’s work where it is needed most, ensuring we can continue essential work in 70 countries and respond to unexpected crises.

If you are raising funds for the Israel Emergency or Ukraine Emergency, this money will be allocated to support JDC’s response to the massive humanitarian needs of the hardest-hit in your chosen location.
