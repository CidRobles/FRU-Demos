About

April 16 — May 7, 2025 is the John Jay Athletics Giving Challenge! This is an exciting opportunity for Bloodhounds—past, present, and honorary—to share your love for your favorite varsity teams with a generous donation and connect with other John Jay sports enthusiasts and supporters.

Because of your unwavering Bloodhound pride and investment over the past decade, John Jay Athletics has transformed into a leading program in the City University of New York Athletic Conference, boasting 15 varsity teams and 225 extraordinary student-athletes. In the last calendar year, the program has captured championships in baseball, men’s basketball, cheerleading, women’s soccer, softball, and women’s volleyball, and we launched our first-ever Esports team, engaging over 100 students in competitive video gaming.

With your partnership, we can reach our goal of $25,000. All donations matter and propel our teams to the next level in team travel opportunities, technology, and state-of-the-art equipment.

Give today and power our Athletics Giving Challenge with your inspiring support.

Go Bloodhounds!
