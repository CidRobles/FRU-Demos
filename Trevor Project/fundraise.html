Reach Out

We strive to end suicide among LGBTQ youth through five key programs.

Crisis Services

The Trevor Project operates 24/7 crisis services across the U.S. and Mexico.

Learn More

Advocacy

We work at the local, state, and federal levels to advocate for legislation that protects the rights of LGBTQ people.

Learn More

Research

Evaluations and surveys help us reduce risk of suicide and better understand the mental health issues affecting LGBTQ youth.

Learn More

Peer Support

TrevorSpace is the world’s largest safe space social networking site for LGBTQ young people ages 13-24 years old.

Learn More

Education and Public Awareness

We educate adults who interact with youth on LGBTQ competent suicide prevention, risk detection, and response.

Learn More
